# USC Pool Hours Calendar

A self-updating calendar of **lap swim hours at the Uytengsu Aquatics Center** (USC University Park Campus).

USC Recreational Sports publishes rec swim hours one week at a time, and they change every week. This calendar checks the official schedule several times a day and keeps itself up to date, so you can see when the pool is open without visiting the website.

## What's on it

Every open lap swim session for both pools at Uytengsu:

- 🏊 **Comp Pool** — the competition pool
- 🤿 **Dive Pool** — the dive pool, which is usually open more often

Events are marked **free**, not busy, so subscribing will not make you look unavailable to anyone who checks your schedule.

All times are **America/Los_Angeles** and adjust for daylight saving automatically.

---

## Good to know

- **Lanes are first-come, first-served.** An open session on the calendar means lanes are available for recreational lap swimming — it is not a reservation.
- **Hours change weekly**, and USC only publishes about seven days ahead. The calendar will normally show roughly one week into the future, not a full month.
- **USC can close the pool on short notice** for events, maintenance, athletics, or holidays. This calendar follows the published schedule, so a last-minute closure may not appear in time.
- Before making a special trip, it never hurts to check the official page.
